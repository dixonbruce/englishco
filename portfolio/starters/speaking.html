—Speaking Part 1 – point and place cards⌄

I can identify objects and put cards in the right place.

Point to the tree. / Put the fish under the table.

Study & practise

Listen to the object and position, then point or move the card.

How to demonstrate mastery

Identify objects in a scene and place three cards as instructed.

Progress unavailable

—Speaking Part 2 – answer scene questions⌄

I can answer questions about objects, colours, numbers and actions.

How many ducks are there? / What is she doing?

Study & practise

Look at the part of the picture the examiner indicates. Give a clear short answer.

How to demonstrate mastery

Answer six questions about a new scene.

Progress unavailable

—Speaking Part 2 – tell me about it⌄

I can say something simple about a person or object.

It’s red. It’s on the chair.

Study & practise

Use a colour, size, position or action to add a detail.

How to demonstrate mastery

Give relevant information about three pictured objects.

Progress unavailable

—Speaking Part 3 – answer object-card questions⌄

I can identify an object and answer a related question.

What is it? A bike. / Have you got a bike? Yes.

Study & practise

Name the object, then listen to the follow-up question about it.

How to demonstrate mastery

Answer questions on four different object cards.

Progress unavailable

—Speaking Part 4 – answer personal questions⌄

I can talk briefly about my family, school and favourite things.

My favourite sport is tennis. / I have one brother.

Study & practise

Listen to the question word and give a relevant answer. Short answers are fine.

How to demonstrate mastery

Answer six varied personal questions without a script.

Progress unavailable

—Ask for repetition⌄

I can ask someone to say something again.

Sorry? / Again, please.

Study & practise

If you do not understand, ask politely rather than staying silent.

How to demonstrate mastery

Use an appropriate request in two role-play situations.

—Clear pronunciation⌄

I can say familiar words clearly enough to be understood.

three / green / shoes

Study & practise

Listen to a model, repeat the word and pay attention to its sounds and stress.

How to demonstrate mastery

Say familiar words and short answers intelligibly in a conversation.
