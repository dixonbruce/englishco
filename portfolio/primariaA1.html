Year 1 (A1) English Portfolio

Our Year 1 course follows a spiral curriculum. Instead of studying a topic once, students revisit grammar and vocabulary throughout the year, gradually building confidence, accuracy and fluency.

29 Grammar Objectives 32 Vocabulary Objectives Spiral Curriculum Continuous Assessment

How the Portfolio Works

Each objective has its own learning page with explanations, examples, speaking practice and revision activities.

Students revisit objectives throughout the year until they can use them confidently in real communication.

Spiral Curriculum

Objectives are revisited regularly throughout the year rather than taught once and forgotten.

Continuous Assessment

Students are assessed through speaking, listening, reading and writing activities.

Mastery Learning

An objective is only marked as mastered when it can be used confidently in real communication.
