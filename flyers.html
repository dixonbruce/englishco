Plan de Estudios: Nivel A2 (Flyers)

Desglose completo del currículo gramatical y temático para el nivel A2 (Cambridge English: Flyers) adaptado a educación primaria.

1. Currículo Gramatical Completo (A2 Grammar)

El nivel A2 consolida la comunicación autónoma. El alumnado pasa de frases aisladas a conectar ideas, relatar eventos pasados, predecir el futuro y expresar hipótesis, condiciones y opiniones.

Tiempos y Formas Verbales

Estructura Gramatical Uso y Regla Ejemplo de Aula
Present Simple vs. Continuous Hábitos, verdades generales y rutinas vs. acciones en progreso o arreglos futuros cercanos. He plays tennis on Tuesdays, but today he is playing football.
Past Simple (Regular & Irregular) Acciones completadas en el pasado con expresiones de tiempo (yesterday, last week, ago). We bought a new puppy two days ago.
Past Continuous Acciones en desarrollo en el pasado y acciones interrumpidas por el Past Simple (when / while). They were watching TV when the lights went out.
Present Perfect Experiencias pasadas sin tiempo específico y acciones con relevancia presente (ever, never, just, already, yet). Have you ever ridden a camel? / She has just finished her project.
Futuro: Be going to Planes intencionados, evidencia inmediata e intenciones previas. Look at those dark clouds, it’s going to rain!
Futuro: Will / Won't Predicciones, promesas y decisiones espontáneas. I think people will live on Mars in the future.
Passive Voice (Present & Past) Voz pasiva básica para centrarse en el objeto o la acción (is/are + participle, was/were + participle). Paper is made from trees. / The museum was built in 1920.
Gerunds & Infinitives Verbos seguidos de -ing (gustos/hobbies) o infinitivo con to (intenciones/necesidades). She enjoys swimming, but she wants to learn how to surf.

Verbos Modales y Auxiliares

Modal Función Ejemplo de Aula
Can / Could Habilidad presente/pasada y peticiones formales o permisos. Could you open the window, please? / I couldn't swim when I was four.
Must / Mustn't Obligación fuerte y prohibición. You mustn't touch the exhibits in the museum.
Have to / Don't have to Obligación externa y ausencia de obligación. We have to wear a uniform, but we don't have to wear a tie.
Should / Shouldn't Consejos y sugerencias. You should drink more water when it's hot.
Might / May Posibilidad en el presente o futuro. We might go to the beach tomorrow if it's sunny.
Shall Ofrecimientos y sugerencias espontáneas. Shall I help you carry those books?

Sintaxis, Oraciones y Determinantes

Estructura Uso / Regla Ejemplo de Aula
First Conditional Condiciones reales en el presente/futuro y sus consecuencias (If + Present, Will + Verb). If you practice every day, you will pass the exam easily.
Zero Conditional Hechos científicos y verdades absolutas (If/When + Present, Present). If you heat ice, it melts.
Relative Clauses Oraciones subordinadas adjetivas (who, which, where, that, whose). That’s the girl who won the spelling bee.
Comparatives & Superlatives Comparaciones de adjetivos/adverbios cortos, largos e irregulares (good/better/best, bad/worse/worst, far/further/furthest). This is the most exciting book I’ve ever read!
Indefinite Pronouns Pronombres indefinidos de persona, lugar y cosa (someone, anything, nowhere, everybody). Is there anything inside the box? No, nobody is here.
